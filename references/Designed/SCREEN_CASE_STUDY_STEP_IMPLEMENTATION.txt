1. SCREEN PURPOSE

Case Study Step is the internal chapter screen for a single case study.

It has two distinct navigation levels:

CASE-LEVEL NAVIGATION:
right-side ui-case-study-rail
switches between different case studies

STEP-LEVEL NAVIGATION:
bottom horizontal case-study-step-rail
switches between chapters/steps inside the current case study

These two navigation systems must remain conceptually and visually separate.

The screen should:
- make the current case unmistakable
- make the current step unmistakable
- provide one strong evidence image
- provide enough room for actual explanatory text
- expose the complete internal step structure without leaving the page
- remain dense but readable on desktop
