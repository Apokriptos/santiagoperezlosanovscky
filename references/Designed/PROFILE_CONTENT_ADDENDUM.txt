## My Approach

## Selected Experience

**Unreal Engine Toolset**

Practical experience with Animation Blueprints, Sequencer and Control Rig within a broader Blueprint-heavy workflow.

**Gameplay Prototyping**

Use animation systems as part of playable prototypes and technical implementation rather than as isolated presentation work.

## My Approach

**Start from gameplay state.**  
Animation logic should respond clearly to what the character and game systems are doing.

**Keep transitions readable.**  
State changes should support both player feedback and implementation clarity.

**Use procedural tools where they earn their cost.**  
Technical animation should solve recurring gameplay problems rather than add complexity for its own sake.

**Treat animation as a system.**  
Character motion works best when animation, controls, AI and gameplay logic are designed together.

Do not position this as traditional character-animation expertise.
